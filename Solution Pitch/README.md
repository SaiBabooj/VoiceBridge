# VoiceBridge — Solution Pitch

**A voice-first travel companion for people who do not share a language.**

## The problem

A traveller lands in Chennai speaking only Hindi. His auto driver speaks only Tamil. Translation
apps assume typing and handing a phone back and forth, phrasebooks are static, and nothing records
what was agreed. Price, stops and pickup time stay verbal — and easy to dispute.

## The solution

VoiceBridge turns the phone into a shared interpreter and a shared contract.

- **Speak naturally.** Each side talks in their own language and is read and heard in the other.
- **See the same thread.** The original stays underneath, with a replay button.
- **Plan together.** Price, hours and stops become one shared day plan.
- **Lock only on agreement.** Nothing is final until both sides confirm.

## The scenario

8:30 AM. Sai chooses Hindi in VoiceBridge and connects with Ravi Kumar, an auto driver nearby.

1. Sai speaks Hindi: “नमस्ते! मैं साई हूँ। मुझे कपालेश्वर मंदिर और मरीना बीच जाना है।” Ravi reads and
   hears it in Tamil.
2. Ravi replies in Tamil: “வணக்கம் சாய்! நான் ரவி. காலை ஒன்பது மணிக்கு வருகிறேன்.” Sai reads and
   hears it in Hindi.
3. Sai asks the day rate; Ravi quotes ₹1,800 including the temple, the beach and dinner.
4. VoiceBridge drafts the plan: 9:00 AM pickup, Kapaleeshwarar Temple, Marina Beach, dinner, ₹1,800.
5. Sai confirms; Ravi sees the same plan and confirms. Both screens show the locked agreement.

Two minutes, two languages, one agreement.

## What makes it different

- **Two-sided by design** — the driver's surface is a first-class product, not an afterthought.
- **Agreement, not just translation** — the outcome is a locked plan both sides can point to.
- **Built for the road** — large touch targets, high contrast, eyes-free playback, and an installable
  PWA with an offline shell in English, Hindi and Tamil.

## Built today

Traveller and driver flows, live Gnani speech recognition and text-to-speech in three languages,
two-sided confirmation and locked agreements. The Chennai conversation ships as a scripted scenario;
transcription and speech are live through the gateway. Next: production translation for free-form
conversation, live driver matching, streaming audio, digital receipts and fleet pilots.

## Why it matters

Fair prices in writing, a plan both sides understand, and the confidence to say yes.
